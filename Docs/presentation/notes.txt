"Regarding latency: We decouple the high-rate control loop at 1000 Hz from the 3D pipeline. Both arbitrary reslicing and ray attenuation are formulated as pure GPU tensor kernels that execute in under 5 milliseconds, while active view planning runs asynchronously at 2 to 5 Hz on downsampled volumes.
Regarding tissue parameters: We do not hand-tune parameters per patient. Standard medical physics allows us to map CT Hounsfield Units directly to physical density and acoustic impedance. Furthermore, by randomizing attenuation and speckle coefficients within physiological ranges during simulation training, we ensure that our AI policies learn to be robust against patient-to-patient variability."

"While SonoGym makes valuable contributions to fast 2D visual servoing in IsaacLab, it simplifies the problem by assuming an external controller perfectly glues the probe to the skin and uses a GAN that bypasses acoustic ray propagation. In contrast, our pipeline couples physical contact mechanics to GPU acoustic ray tracing, builds a 3D probabilistic entropy map, and uses active Next-Best-View planning to look around acoustic obstacles. We treat ultrasound as an active 3D information-gathering task rather than a passive 2D image-following task."
