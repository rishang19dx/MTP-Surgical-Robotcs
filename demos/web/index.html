Recorded in 3D Slicer and PyBullet

The same simulator driving both programs, frame by frame. Top: 3D Slicer with the body cut open at the probe's image plane, the live B-mode on the cut face and the force plot. Bottom: PyBullet with the displacement bands, the skin profile at rest (white) and pressed (orange), and the force arrow.

What is computed. Each scene holds exact FEM solutions on a grid of probe positions along the path and forces of 1, 3.5 and 6 N; between grid points the page interpolates bilinearly. The force is the reaction force of the tissue on the probe face, found by bisection on the indentation. Displacements are shown at true scale unless magnified.

Model and limits. Linear tetrahedral FEM with precomputed Green's functions, 2 mm elements, shared tissue stiffness with the fat fitted to a popliteal-vein compression measurement (3.4 kPa). Small-strain physics, no hyperelasticity; the B-mode is a model-based simulation from CT, not a recorded scan. Source CT: SonoGym / TotalSegmentator (CC BY 4.0).
